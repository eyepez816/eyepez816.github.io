🧭 What This Application Does

Appcode Desktop is a desktop utility built for one clear job: keeping your Appcode data folders in a safe place on your own computer. Software updates and patches can quietly change how your config files and export files are stored. When that happens, older data can get overwritten, renamed, or lost.

This utility steps in before a patch is applied. It looks at your Appcode data folders, takes a dated copy of the config and export files inside them, and writes that copy to disk. You end up with a tidy history of snapshots you can open at any time, using nothing more than your normal file browser.

There is no account to create, no cloud service to subscribe to, and no complex interface to learn. Appcode Desktop stays out of the way and simply makes sure that the files you rely on are preserved before anything changes.

🗂️ How the Dated Copies Are Stored

Each time a patch is applied, Appcode Desktop creates a new folder named with the date and time of the copy. Inside that folder, your config and export files are arranged exactly as they were, so you can find any file by its original path.

Because the folders are named in a sortable date-and-time order, the newest copy always appears at the bottom of the list and the oldest at the top. You can open any of them with File Explorer, copy a single file out of a snapshot, or move an entire snapshot to an external drive for extra safety.

⏱️ What Happens Before a Patch

Right before a patch is applied, Appcode Desktop performs a short, predictable routine:

  • It checks that your Appcode data folder is still reachable and readable.
  • It reads the list of config files and export files currently in place.
  • It creates a new dated folder for this specific moment.
  • It copies the files into that folder without altering the originals.
  • It verifies that the copy is complete and matches what was read.
  • It records the result so you can see later exactly when the copy was made.

If any part of the routine cannot be completed, the app stops and tells you plainly what went wrong, rather than leaving you with a partial copy you cannot trust.

🧰 Everyday Use

Most of the time you will not interact with Appcode Desktop at all. It sits in the background and does its job. When you do want to check on it, open the app and you will see a simple list of snapshots with their dates and sizes, plus a status line telling you whether your data folder is being watched as expected.

Three tasks cover almost everything you will ever need:

  • Take a copy now. Create a snapshot immediately, without waiting for a patch, before you try something risky.
  • Open a snapshot. Jump straight to a dated folder in File Explorer to look at what was saved.
  • Clean up old copies. Remove snapshots you no longer need so the backup folder does not grow without limit.

🔒 Privacy and Where Your Data Lives

Everything Appcode Desktop does happens on your own computer. Your config and export files are read from your disk and written back to your disk. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is synced to an online service, and nothing leaves your machine.

The backup folder lives wherever you choose to put it. Many people keep it on the same drive as their Appcode data for convenience; others point it at an external drive or a folder that is already covered by their own backup routine. Either approach works, and you can change the location at any time from the app's settings.

🧪 Tips for a Smooth Experience

  • Keep the backup folder on a drive with enough free space for several snapshots, especially if your export files are large.
  • Point the backup folder at a different drive than your Appcode data if you want protection against a single drive failing.
  • Take a manual snapshot before any change you consider risky, even if no patch is involved.
  • Check the snapshot list once a month and clear out anything you no longer need.
  • Leave the app running in the background so it can catch a patch the moment it begins.
